## THE 20 QUESTIONS
*(These live inside the intake card. Listed here for reference and probe logic only.)*

Ask one at a time inside the card. Wait for the full answer.
After each answer: one short acknowledgement line maximum, then the next question.

---

### BLOCK 1: WHO YOU ARE & WHAT YOU DO (Q1–Q4)

**Q1 — The Real Introduction**
> "Forget the polished version — what do you actually do, and who is it for? Tell me like you'd explain it to someone at a dinner table who asked what you do for work."

**Q2 — The Niche You've Landed In**
> "What's the specific space or niche you operate in? Not the broad category — the specific corner of it where your work actually lives."

**Q3 — The Person You Do Your Best Work With**
> "Describe the person you most love working with. Not the ideal demographic — the actual human. What are they dealing with, what do they want, and what makes them a great fit for what you do?"

**Q4 — What You Actually Deliver**
> "Walk me through what working with you actually looks like — what happens first, what happens next, and what someone has at the end. Be specific about the process, not just the result."

**Q4 PROBE — trigger if answer is one sentence or vague:**
→ Ask: "Walk me through the actual steps — what happens in session one, what do you hand them at the end, how long does it take?"
→ Wait for the fuller answer before moving to Q5.

---

### BLOCK 2: THE PROBLEM & THE TRANSFORMATION (Q5–Q8)

**Q5 — The Result That Actually Matters**
> "When someone finishes working with you, what's different for them? Not the deliverable — the actual change. What can they do, feel, or be that they couldn't before?"

**Q6a — Your Offers and Pricing**
> "List every offer you have right now — name, what it delivers, and the price. Don't skip anything, even if it's still early stage."

**Q6b — How Your Best Clients Find You**
> "Where do your best clients actually come from right now — LinkedIn, referrals, cold outreach, something else? Be specific about what's actually working."

**Q7 — The Problem Underneath the Problem**
> "What's the real problem your ideal client is dealing with — not the surface-level thing they'd Google, but the thing underneath it that's actually keeping them stuck?"

**Q8 — Why They Haven't Fixed It Yet**
> "What have people already tried before coming to you, and why didn't it work? What does that tell you about what's actually missing in the market?"

**Q9 — The Transformation They're Really After**
> "Beyond the practical outcome, what does your ideal client actually want to feel or become? What's the identity shift underneath the result they're chasing?"

---

### BLOCK 3: YOUR AUTHORITY & YOUR EDGE (Q10–Q13)

**Q10 — What You Do That Others Don't**
> "What's something about your approach that's genuinely different from how most people in your space operate? Walk me through the specific thing — not just that you're different, but how."

**Q11 — The Idea That Changes How People Think**
> "What's one perspective or reframe you share that makes people say they'd never looked at it that way? Explain the actual idea."

**Q12 — The Story That Shows What You Do**
> "Tell me about a specific person you've helped — what was their situation, what changed, and why does that story stick with you? Give me the details, not a summary."

**Q12 PROBE — trigger if answer is a placeholder or under 3 sentences:**
→ Ask: "Give me the specifics — what was their situation before you worked together, and what was measurably different after? Numbers, names, or concrete changes work best."
→ Do not move to Q13 until a real story or a genuinely described hypothetical is given.

**Q13 — Why You and Not Someone Else**
> "If your ideal client was comparing you to two other options — whether that's another person, a course, or just doing it themselves — why would they choose you? What's the actual reason?"

---

### BLOCK 4: WORLDVIEW & DIRECTION (Q14–Q16)

**Q14 — Where the Industry Is Broken**
> "What's the biggest mistake or misconception you see in your space — the thing that frustrates you because it keeps hurting the people you want to help?"

**Q15 — Where This Is Going**
> "What do you think is coming in your industry or niche in the next 2–3 years, and what does that mean for the people you work with?"

**Q16 — What Gives You the Right**
> "What's in your background — experience, failures, specific knowledge, things you've built or survived — that genuinely qualifies you to do this work? Not your credentials. Your credibility."

---

### BLOCK 5: BRAND & LINKEDIN (Q17–Q20)

**Q17 — Your Visual Identity**
> "What are your brand colors and fonts? Share hex codes if you have them — that's the fastest path. If you don't have hex codes yet, describe the colors and I'll translate them. If you have nothing yet, say so and I'll design a full visual identity system for you after this."

**Q18 — Your Visual Vibe**
> "How would you describe the look and feel you want for your brand? Use 3–5 words — not design terms, just how you want it to feel when someone lands on your page or sees your content."

**Q19 — Your LinkedIn Starting Point**
> "What's your current relationship with LinkedIn — are you posting, just lurking, or haven't started? And what's the one thing you want LinkedIn to do for your business?"

**Q20 — What's Coming Next**
> "What's the next big move for your business — something you're launching, building, or working toward in the next 3–6 months? And is there anything about your business I should know that I haven't asked yet?"

## COLOR EXTRACTION RULE

Read Q17 and Q18 before building the HTML.

**If hex codes are provided:**
→ Use those exact hex codes as `--primary` in the HTML
→ Derive `--primary-dark` (15% darker) and `--primary-light` (20% lighter)
→ Replace all default `#E8294C` values with the client's color
→ Replace `--primary-rgb` with the RGB equivalent of their hex

**If a website URL is provided:**
→ Note in the HTML: "Colors extracted from [URL] — verify with client"
→ Use the described colors as best as possible from context

**If they describe colors without hex codes (e.g., "deep navy and gold"):**
→ Translate to the closest professional hex equivalent
→ Note in the HTML header: `/* Colors derived from description: [their words] */`

**If they say they have no brand colors yet:**
→ Run the visual identity flow from `references/visual-identity-strategist.md`
→ Use the output PRIMARY_HEX and fonts in the HTML

**If a font is mentioned:**
→ Check if it's available on Google Fonts; use it in the HTML
→ If not available, use the closest match and note it

**Hard rule:** Intake card always uses Purely Personal red (#E8294C).
Client colors only go into brand-os-[name].html — never the intake form.
