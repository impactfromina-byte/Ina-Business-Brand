## SECTION 3: THE CORE PROBLEM THEY WANT SOLVED

**Problem 1:** "I've achieved a lot — but I don't know what comes next. And I don't even know what I want anymore."
*(The achievement-without-direction trap: they've been executing so well for so long that stopping to choose was never on the agenda.)*

**Problem 2:** "I'm so overworked and frustrated that I can't even look left or right. My head isn't free to think about something new."
*(The burned-out high performer: the will to move is there, but capacity to see options has collapsed.)*

**Problem 3:** "I want to become a leader / go freelance / switch departments — but I don't actually know what that means from the inside."
*(The title trap: they're chasing a role that sounds right but haven't been shown what it actually requires.)*

**Why they haven't solved it yet:**
They've tried career books, workshops, generic coaching, online courses — and found that none of it translates to how companies actually work. The coaches didn't know what really happens behind closed doors. The books were theory. AI-written applications got nowhere. What they were missing is someone who has been inside, has seen how decisions really get made, and can tell them the truth — fast.

---

## SECTION 4: WHAT THEY WANT INSTEAD

**Tangible outcome:** A concrete next move — job offer, promotion, freelance launch, business founded, or department transferred — with a plan that actually works in the real world.

**Emotional outcome:** The relief of finally having a clear head. The confidence to make a decision and commit to it. The feeling of being back in the driver's seat.

**Identity outcome:** From "high achiever who somehow got stuck" to "person who takes ownership of their own path — no matter what."

## SECTION 7: ICP PERSONA SUMMARY

The ideal client for Impact von Ina is a high-achieving professional — typically mid to senior level — who has spent years delivering results for their employer and has quietly stopped knowing what they themselves actually want. They're not failing; they're often performing well. But they feel a creeping sense of being trapped: overworked, under-energised, and unable to imagine a next chapter. They're not whiners looking for someone to blame — they're doers who've temporarily lost direction and want someone to help them find it fast, with real advice that translates to how organisations actually work. They fund this investment themselves, which means they're serious. And they respond to someone who's been inside, who knows the system, and who won't waste their time.

---

## SECTION 8: CONTENT DIRECTION FROM ICP

- **Topics that resonate:** The truth about how promotions actually happen. What corporate culture really means for your career. How to make a decision when you're too tired to think. The difference between the title you want and the job it actually is. Why your last coaching didn't work.
- **Tone that connects:** Direct. Warm. No bullshit. The colleague who's seen it all and will tell you what nobody else will.
- **Proof they need:** Specific examples from real companies and real situations — not anonymised frameworks. The sales manager who became a manager and hated it. The controller who ended up in endless meetings. Real.
- **What to avoid saying:** "Empower." "Unlock your potential." "Holistic." "Journey." Any language that sounds like a course landing page from 2019.
