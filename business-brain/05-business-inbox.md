### Results & Case Stories

**Story 1 — The Sales Leader Who Became a Manager:**
Before: senior sales professional eager to move into leadership with a team. What was done: clarified what the management role actually involves day-to-day (Excel, forecasting, internal meetings) versus what he had been doing (direct client contact, deals, relationships). After: made an informed decision — either to pursue leadership with full awareness of what it meant, or to reposition toward a senior individual contributor path that played to his actual strengths. Why it matters: prevented a costly, demoralising mistake made by going in blind.

**Story 2 — The Controller Who Ended Up in Strategy:**
Before: finance expert who became a manager — and found himself in endless strategy meetings instead of the numbers work he actually loved. What was done: named the mismatch between the role's reality and his actual motivation; mapped what a more aligned path looked like. After: clarity on what he wanted from the next move — and what to look for / negotiate before taking a leadership role again.
