## SECTION 1: ORIGIN & FOUNDATION

### The Moment That Changed Everything

Ina's turning point wasn't one dramatic moment — it crystallized during an internship in a training/development department. Confronted with people passively waiting on the state for solutions, and colleagues who left the office at exactly 6pm saying you didn't need a degree for this work, she made a clear decision: "No, this isn't for me. I want to go into business, where money is made and earned."

In the same internship, she discovered Vera F. Birkenbihl. The first video — on "pragmatic esotericism" — opened her eyes to cause-and-effect relationships and the role her own mindset played in shaping what happened around her. A gifted trainer who broke down complex psychological concepts so that anyone could use them — this became Ina's lifelong professional role model.

### The Person Who Shaped You

Vera F. Birkenbihl — trainer, seminar leader, author, speaker — fundamentally shaped Ina's professional approach. What stuck wasn't technical terminology, but translation: making the complex simple without diluting the content. Impact through clarity, not through showmanship.

Ina encountered Birkenbihl only through videos and books — never in person.

---

## SECTION 2: VALUES & PHILOSOPHY

**Core values:** Freedom, self-reliance, independence. Ina has walked away from major safety nets twice in her career, because freedom consistently outweighed security in her decision-making.

**Professional philosophy:** Translate complex knowledge into plain, immediately actionable language — without jargon, without diluting the substance. This is the direct legacy of Birkenbihl's influence.

**Definition of success:** Has evolved over the years — from driven, exhausting performance to "when success comes with ease, it's done right."

---

## CLOSING PORTRAIT

Ina has always known what she wants — even when the path to it required courage, loss, and cost. She has walked away from two major safety nets because freedom consistently outweighs security in her calculus. She works without pose, translates complexity into clarity, and helps people find the ground under their feet again.

Her definition of success has shifted — from driven performance to earned ease.

What she delivers isn't a deliverable. It's a new self-image.
