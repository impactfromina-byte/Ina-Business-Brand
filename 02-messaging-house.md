## FOUNDATION

**The Central Tension:**
High performers have been so focused on achieving what was expected of them that they've never stopped to decide what they actually want. Now they're stuck — successful by most measures, directionless by their own. They have the drive, the experience, and the credentials. What they don't have is someone who can tell them the truth about how the system works and help them move through it faster.

**The Unique Messaging Angle:**
Ina has been inside the room. Not as a recruiter seeing the outside, not as a trainer delivering frameworks from a manual — as the HR executive who coached the leaders, managed the restructurings, watched promotions get made and blocked, and saw exactly why people advance or stall. That insider access is the product. Nobody else in the career coaching space can honestly claim it at this level of scope and depth.

## UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION

**Short (12 words):**
Career clarity and a real plan — from someone who's been inside.

**Medium (30 words):**
Ina Spuling combines 22+ years of corporate HR leadership with executive coaching to help high performers cut through the noise, understand how the system actually works, and make their next move with confidence.

**Long (65 words):**
Most career coaches give you frameworks. Ina gives you the truth. After 22+ years inside companies of every size — advising leaders, shaping cultures, watching decisions get made — she knows what actually moves the needle for people who've already proved themselves. She works with high performers who are stuck, burned out, or unsure of what's next, and gets them to a clear decision and a working plan — fast, practical, and no nonsense.

*UVP test: Could a generic career coach say this? No. The specific claim of 22+ years inside HR — coaching leaders, managing restructurings, witnessing how promotions actually happen — is not replicable.*

## MESSAGING PILLARS

### Pillar 1: "The people who advise you on corporate life should have actually lived it."
- **Why it matters:** The client's core frustration is advice that doesn't translate to reality. They've been burned by theory that didn't work in their actual company, with their actual boss.
- **Proof point:** Ina has worked across companies of every size, in every function from admin to VP/Interim Head, in one of the most fast-moving environments (IT/Cyber Security).
- **In her words:** "Ein Personaler, der ist drin. Der wird mit dem Problem konfrontiert."

### Pillar 2: "You're not stuck because you're not capable. You're stuck because nobody showed you how it really works."
- **Why it matters:** Removes self-blame. Reframes the stuckness from personal failure to system opacity — which Ina can decode.
- **Proof point:** Sales leaders who became managers and discovered Excel-filled days. Controllers who ended up in strategy meetings instead of the numbers they love.
- **In her words:** "Dass das gar nicht an ihnen liegt und ihrem Know-how, sondern dass viele interne Mechanismen dafür verantwortlich sind."

### Pillar 3: "If you want to move, you have to take responsibility for the move."
- **Why it matters:** Ina doesn't position herself as the rescuer. The insight and the plan come from her — the decision and the action have to come from the client. This keeps the relationship adult-to-adult, not dependent.
- **Proof point:** Her coaching philosophy throughout the intake and prior sessions: personal responsibility is the non-negotiable variable. Everything else — training, advice, opportunity — is support; the person has to move.
- **In her words:** "Jeder ist seines Glückes Schmied."

## BRAND VOICE GUIDELINES

**Voice attributes:**

1. **Direct** — Says the thing plainly. No warming-up, no softening. Do: "Here's what's actually happening." Don't: "Well, it's complex and there are many perspectives to consider..."

2. **Warm** — Genuine care for the person, not the process. Do: Make them feel seen and understood before challenging them. Don't: Clinical, transactional, or cold even when being sharp.

3. **Insider** — Speaks with the authority of someone who has been inside. Do: Use specific corporate situations, real dynamics, named patterns. Don't: Speak in theory or from frameworks alone.

4. **No-nonsense** — Cuts what doesn't serve. Do: Short, specific, actionable. Don't: Long wind-ups, excessive caveating, polished marketing language.

5. **Umsetzungsstark** — Oriented toward action and results. Do: End every conversation with something concrete. Don't: Leave things open-ended or philosophical.

**Words to use:**
Klarheit — Verantwortung — Insider-Know-how — Mechanismen — Shortcut — Macher — Pragmatisch — Resultate — Konkret — Entscheidung treffen — Durchsetzung — Ehrlich — Schnell — Praktisch

**Words to avoid:**
Transformation — Journey — Empower — Leverage — Unlock — Holistic — Ecosystem — Navigate — Synergy — Ganzheitlich — Potenzial entfalten — Augenhöhe (overused in German coaching) — any language that sounds like a personal development brochure
